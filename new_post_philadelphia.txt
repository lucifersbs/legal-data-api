<h2>Philadelphia's Unique Slip and Fall Risks</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Weather-Related Hazards</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Philadelphia experiences all four seasons, each bringing specific slip and fall risks. Winter snow and ice, spring rain, summer humidity, and fall leaves all create hazardous walking conditions. Property owners must address these seasonal hazards promptly to prevent accidents.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Historic Infrastructure</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Philadelphia's historic nature means many buildings and sidewalks are decades or even centuries old. Uneven brick sidewalks, worn steps, aging buildings, and historic properties present unique maintenance challenges and potential hazards for pedestrians.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>High-Density Urban Environment</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Center City, Old City, and Philadelphia's numerous neighborhoods see heavy foot traffic daily. Busy restaurants, shops, office buildings, and tourist destinations must maintain safe conditions despite constant use.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2>Pennsylvania Premises Liability Law</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Pennsylvania law categorizes visitors and establishes different duties of care:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Invitees</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Business visitors and customers are owed the highest duty of care. Property owners must inspect for dangers, repair known hazards, and provide warnings of dangerous conditions.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Licensees</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Social guests are entitled to warnings of known dangerous conditions.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Trespassers</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Property owners owe no duty to adult trespassers except to refrain from willful or wanton misconduct, with exceptions for children.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2>Proving Liability in Philadelphia Slip and Fall Cases</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>To recover compensation, your Philadelphia slip and fall attorney must prove:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul>
<li>A dangerous condition existed</li>
<li>The property owner knew or should have known about the hazard</li>
<li>The owner failed to take reasonable steps to address it</li>
<li>The condition caused your injuries</li>
<li>You suffered damages</li>
</ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Pennsylvania follows a modified comparative negligence rule. You can recover damages if you were 50% or less at fault, but your recovery will be reduced by your percentage of fault. If you're 51% or more at fault, you cannot recover.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul>
<li><strong>Seek medical attention:</strong> Document injuries immediately.</li>
<li><strong>Report the accident:</strong> Notify property management and request a written incident report.</li>

<li><strong>Collect witness information:</strong> Get contact details from anyone who saw your fall.</li>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>How long do I have to file a slip and fall lawsuit in Pennsylvania?</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Pennsylvania has a two-year statute of limitations for most slip and fall cases. Claims against government entities have shorter notice requirements. Contact an attorney immediately.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>What if I fell on a Philadelphia sidewalk?</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Sidewalk liability depends on whether the adjacent property is commercial or residential. Commercial property owners are generally responsible for adjacent sidewalks. Claims against the City of Philadelphia have special notice requirements.</p>
