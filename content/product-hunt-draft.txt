FULL DESCRIPTION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
**What is it?**
Legal Data API provides comprehensive US legal data through a simple REST API. Perfect for developers building legal tech tools, law firm websites, case management systems, and settlement calculators.

**Key Features:**
✅ 20+ API endpoints
✅ All 50 US states + DC covered
✅ Statute of limitations by case type
✅ Damage caps and punitive limits
✅ Average settlement ranges
✅ Jury verdict data
✅ Court filing fees and deadlines
✅ Comparative negligence rules
✅ Attorney and expert witness fees
✅ Legal citations and precedents

**Use Cases:**
• Legal tech startups building case management tools
• Law firms automating intake forms
• Developers creating settlement calculators
• Legal researchers needing structured data
• SaaS founders in the legal space

**Pricing:**
• Free: 100 requests/month
• Basic: $19/month (10,000 requests)
• Pro: $79/month (50,000 requests)

**Why I built it:**
As a developer interested in legal tech, I spent weeks compiling legal data for a side project. I realized every legal tech developer faces this same problem. So I built the API I wished existed.

**Tech Stack:**
Node.js, Express, Render hosting, RapidAPI marketplace

**Roadmap:**
• International expansion (UK, Canada, Australia)
• More data endpoints
• Webhook notifications
• Portfolio risk monitoring
